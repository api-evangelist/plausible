Short answer: Plausible can be blocked, but far less often than other privacy-invasive tools such as Google Analytics. And if missing visits from blockers matter for your site, you can proxy Plausible through your own domain to bypass most of the remaining blocks and get more accurate stats. Plausible is a privacy-friendly , cookieless analytics tool.
